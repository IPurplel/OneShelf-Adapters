# Licence and contribution terms — undecided

The owner has **not** chosen a licence for this repository or its adapters, nor contribution terms (a
licence for contributions, a DCO, a CLA, or copyright assignment). This is recorded as an open owner
decision in OneShelf's records (UD-4 for Core, and its counterpart for this repository).

Until it is decided:

- the default legal position applies — published for reading and review, no rights granted;
- Pull Requests can be opened and reviewed on their technical merits;
- the project does not rely on third-party contributions as redistributable project assets.

Nothing in this repository should be read as choosing terms on the owner's behalf.
